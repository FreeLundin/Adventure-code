# Cultural Review Checklist for New Orleans Folklore Artifacts

Each artifact inspired by New Orleans folklore, voodoo traditions, and local legends must pass a cultural sensitivity review before implementation or public exposure. Sync with design and code reviewers.

- [ ] Identify folkloric inspiration and source material (mythology, region, attributes).
- [ ] Cite at least two reputable sources (books, academic articles, cultural organizations).
- [ ] Describe mechanical effects in game and ensure they respect cultural context.
- [ ] Consult with at least one cultural advisor / community member familiar with the tradition.
- [ ] Include disclaimers in-game if appropriate (e.g., "Inspired by New Orleans folklore").
- [ ] Avoid stereotyping, caricature, or trivialization of spiritual practices.
- [ ] Review artifact name, iconography, and passive/active abilities for cultural appropriateness.
- [ ] Document review outcome and store notes alongside the artifact DataAsset (e.g., as metadata fields).
- [ ] Add cultural review summary to `Design Document/Implementation/Artifact_Notes.md` (or equivalent).

> **Note:** this checklist lives in runbooks so it can be referenced in PRs or by QA during playtests.
